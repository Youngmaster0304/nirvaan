📋 Problem ID: SIH26027 🏛️ Ministry of Railways 💻 Software Category
Problem Statement

The Challenge

Decentralized maintenance planning causes inefficiency and safety risks

⚠️ Current Problems

  • Departments plan maintenance independently - Engineering, Traction, S&T
  • Data silos across TMS, SMMS, TDMS, and COA systems
  • Manual block requests via phone calls - no written records
  • Controllers often refuse blocks, causing maintenance backlog
  • Maintenance corridors printed in timetables are frequently violated

📊 Impact on Operations

  • Reduced asset availability and increased downtime
  • Safety incidents (Khatauli, Kanchanjunga accidents)
  • Signal defects remaining undetected for 3+ years
  • Engineers forced to work without proper blocks
